## What to have ready so we can move fast

If you're chasing a same-day slot, a little prep on your end buys us real time.

- **Know what's going and what's staying.** Sounds obvious. It's the number one thing that slows a job down. If it's ambiguous, put a sticky note on the stuff that stays.
- **Clear the path.** Move cars out of the driveway. Prop the gate. Unlock the garage. If we're carrying a sectional through a hallway, clear the hallway.
- **Sort out parking and access.** In an oceanfront condo building, tell us where the truck can sit and which elevator we're allowed to use. If the front desk needs to buzz us in, give them a heads up.
- **Drain the hot tub.** If we're pulling a spa, get it drained the night before. A full tub is a non-starter for a same-day pickup. Details on our [hot tub removal service](/services/hot-tub-removal).
- **Be reachable.** Same-day means the schedule is moving. Keep your phone on you.

## What we can't do same-day

Two categories, and we'd rather tell you now than waste your afternoon.

**Hazardous materials — ever.** Wet paint, gasoline, propane, asbestos, medical waste, ammunition. We're not licensed to haul any of it and we won't. Those need a proper hazardous waste facility. Dried-out paint cans are usually a different story — ask us.

**Very large multi-day cleanouts.** A full hoarding situation or a whole-estate clearout isn't a "squeeze it into the afternoon" job. Those need real planning — the right size crew, the right number of truckloads, sometimes multiple days. Call us and we'll get you on the calendar properly. Rushing a job like that serves nobody.
